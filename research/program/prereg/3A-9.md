# PREREG 3A-9 - Last-30-minute momentum conditioned on the vol state (dealer short-gamma proxy)
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached day table + CBOE VIX/VIX3M closes. Nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens (JFE 2021) show intraday momentum in the last half hour is driven by
  HEDGING DEMAND - leveraged-ETF end-of-day rebalancing and option dealers who are net short gamma must buy after a rise and sell
  after a fall into the close. That demand scales with the day's move and with how short gamma dealers are, which is highest in
  stressed, high-implied-vol states. The payer is the hedger who must trade at the close regardless of price; the taker of the
  other side in the last 30 minutes is paid. In calm, long-gamma states the flow reverses sign (3A-8's pinning), so the
  unconditional version nets to nothing (as it did in September).
- Source: Baltussen, Da, Lammers, Martens 2021; Gao, Han, Li, Zhou 2018 (intraday momentum); Cheng & Madhavan 2009 (leveraged ETFs).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; 14:30 -> 14:59 CT, flat before 15:00.
- Data: day table; state r = VIX/VIX3M at the PRIOR day's close vs its expanding median computed on strictly earlier dates
  (250-day warm-up; the same construction as 3A-14, verified for look-ahead there). Day move m = o_1430 / o_0830 - 1.
- Rule, exactly: on days in the HIGH state (r_{d-1} >= expanding median), at 14:30 take the position in the direction of sign(m),
  exit at the 14:59 close. No stop. 1 micro. Direction pre-declared: FOLLOW in the high state. One state variable, one threshold.
- Grid (PBO only): {high follow, low follow, high fade, all follow, high & |m| > 50 bp follow}.
- Cost model: fixed $ and §0b bp; both reported.
- Predictions (gross per trade vs the round trip):
  * High-state days ~50% of days: ~125 trades/yr, ~1,650 on ES.
  * High follow: +2 to +4 bp per trade gross ($2-4 per MES trade at 2010-23 levels, < 1x the $4.50 RT); bp rule: net +0.5 to +2.5
    bp, cost share 35-70%. PF 1.03-1.12; Sharpe (annualised by 125) 0.2-0.5; DSR p < 0.95 at N~44. Expected FAIL on DSR/PF,
    with the direction visible: high follow > low follow by >= 2 bp; low follow ~0 or negative; high & big-move follow the
    strongest (4-8 bp) on ~60 trades/yr.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, high-follow series per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 trades.
- What would make me distrust a pass: the edge confined to 2020-03; high follow positive but high & big-move NOT larger (the
  mechanism scales with the move); the low state also positive (then the state is irrelevant); PBO > 0.5.

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=41..44), but the mechanism's fingerprint is there on ES/NQ/YM, in the predicted direction and ordering:
- High-state follow +2.8 bp per trade on ES (NQ +2.6, YM +2.0, RTY +0.4); low-state follow -0.1 / -0.2 / -0.5 / +1.0: the state
  separates the sign exactly as Baltussen et al. predict. High-state AND |day move| > 50 bp: +4.9 bp ES, +3.8 NQ, +3.8 YM
  (the mechanism scales with the move, as pre-registered). High-state fade is the mirror image (-2.8 bp).
- Not tradeable at these costs: gross $2.67 per MES trade vs $4.50 (0.59x) in 2010-23 dollars; bp rule net +1.4 bp ES / +2.0 NQ,
  PF 1.12 / 1.16, cost share 51% / 23%, DSR p 0.18 / 0.33. NQ passes PF and cost share under the bp rule and fails DSR.
  PBO 0.00-0.02 on ES/NQ/YM (the grid ordering is stable), 0.77 on RTY (nothing there).
- Predictions: +2..+4 bp predicted, +2.8 realised; big-move +4..+8, +4.9; low state ~0, -0.1. Right on all counts; verdict FAIL as predicted.
This is the one intraday mechanism in the catalog that behaves as the literature says. ~118 trades/yr at ~2 bp net on NQ is
a $5/trade sleeve in a micro at 2026 levels - real but tiny, and 30 minutes of exposure a day. Retired as a standalone; worth
a line in the Phase 3 portfolio note as a possible low-correlation add-on if the program ever runs full-size NQ.
