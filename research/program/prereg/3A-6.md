# PREREG 3A-6 - Scheduled-macro nights (CPI / NFP / FOMC): does the overnight premium concentrate on them?
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: the cached ES/NQ/RTY/YM nights table (research/program/nights.py), nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the equity premium is compensation for bearing scheduled macro-announcement risk, and it is paid
  mostly ON the announcement (Savor & Wilson 2013: S&P excess return ~11 bp on CPI/NFP/FOMC days vs ~1 bp on other days,
  1958-2009; Lucca & Moench 2015: the FOMC share accrues in the 24h BEFORE the 13:00 CT statement). Payers: hedgers who buy
  protection into the number and intraday desks that will not carry a 07:30 print. The holder of a futures position through
  the release is paid for that gap risk.
- Source: Savor & Wilson, JFQA 2013 "How much do investors care about macroeconomic risk?"; Lucca & Moench, JF 2015
  "The pre-FOMC announcement drift"; Ai & Bansal 2018 (announcement premium theory).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session, identical to 3A-1 (17:02 -> 08:30 CT). CPI/NFP print at 07:30 CT INSIDE the hold;
  the FOMC statement (13:00 CT) is outside it, so the FOMC "night" is the night INTO the decision day (Lucca-Moench window).
- Data: nights table 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; calendars/cpi.csv, nfp.csv (BLS archive file-name dates), fomc.csv (Fed, scheduled
  meetings only). Event night := a night whose EXIT date d1 is a CPI, NFP or scheduled-FOMC decision date. Holdout untouched.
- Rule, exactly: same night as 3A-1 (buy 17:02 open, 1% stop, sell 08:30 open, 1 micro), held ONLY on event nights.
  Direction pre-declared: HOLD INTO the release (the literature says the premium is there, not absent). The complement
  (non-event nights) is reported in notes, not scored.
- Parameters / grid (PBO only): variants = {event nights, non-event nights, CPI-only, NFP-only, FOMC-into-only, post-FOMC night
  (d0 = decision date), all nights}; daily P&L aligned on the full night index with 0 when not held. No headline chosen from it.
- Cost model: fixed (PROGRAM.md §0): $4.50 RT MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM.
- Predictions (the question is gross $/night in the subset vs the round trip):
  * Event nights per symbol: ~430 of ~3470 (12-13%); ~32/year.
  * ES event-night gross: 7-12 bp/night = $6-10 per MES night at the 2010-23 average level 2585, i.e. 1.3-2.2x the $4.50 RT.
    Non-event nights: 1.5-2.5 bp = $1.5-2.5, below the RT (that is where 3A-1's fail came from). NQ event nights 9-15 bp ($8-14 vs
    $3.00 RT, 2.5-4.5x); YM like ES in bp ($4-6 vs $3.00 RT); RTY noisy, 2017+ only.
  * Sharpe of the event-night series, annualised by its own ~32 nights/yr: 0.4-0.8; net PF 1.15-1.40; cost share 40-70% on ES
    (likely a FAIL on cost share even if the premium is there), 20-40% on NQ.
  * Worst event night (MES, 1% stop): -$250 to -$600 (a 2-3% gap on a CPI/NFP print fills at the open through the stop).
  * Decomposition: FOMC-into nights the richest in bp (Lucca-Moench ~ 30-50 bp/day pre-2015, decayed after), NFP next, CPI
    weakest pre-2021 and strongest 2021-23. Post-FOMC night (d0 = decision day): ~0, reported only.
  * Stop grid: the 1% stop should bind more often on event nights (gap-through fills), costing 15-25% of gross.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, on the event-night subset per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95 at the running N, PF >= 1.15,
  cost share < 40%, worst night inside the venue floor. >= 400 nights required: ES/NQ/YM qualify, RTY (~200) does not and is
  reported only.
- What would make me distrust a pass: a pass driven by < 10 nights (2020-03 and 2022 CPI prints); FOMC-into nights showing
  nothing while the subset passes on CPI alone (mechanism mismatch); event nights' bp not exceeding non-event bp (then any pass
  is index-level luck, not the announcement premium); roll nights (quarterly expiry weeks contain an FOMC and a CPI) dominating.

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four by the gate, but the mechanism is confirmed in the direction predicted (SCOREBOARD N=5..8):
- Event nights earn 2-3x the bp of ordinary nights: ES 6.6 bp vs 2.7 (gross $10.38 vs $4.50 RT = 2.31x), NQ 8.6 vs 3.8
  ($13.84 vs $3.00 = 4.6x), YM 5.4 vs 2.2, RTY 15.7 vs 5.3. Net PF 1.24-1.42, Sharpe 0.38-0.61 annualised by ~30 nights/yr.
- Why it fails: DSR p 0.47-0.77 (< 0.95) - ~400 nights of a 6-9 bp effect with gap-night variance is not enough to clear the
  deflated-Sharpe bar at N=5-8; ES cost share 43% (just over 40%). NQ clears cost share (22%) and PF and fails only DSR.
- Decomposition matches Lucca-Moench: the night INTO the FOMC decision is the whole story - ES 14.1 bp ($24/night), NQ 24.6 bp
  ($42/night), YM 12.9 bp, RTY 22.6 bp, 100-108 nights each. NFP is mixed (ES +4.6 bp, NQ -1.4 bp), CPI weak on ES/YM and strong
  on NQ (8.3 bp). The night AFTER the decision is strongly negative everywhere (-15 to -18 bp): a post-FOMC reversal.
- Predictions scored: event-night gross $6-10 predicted, $10.38 realised (top of range); non-event $1.5-2.5 predicted, $2.33;
  cost share 40-70% predicted, 43%; worst night -$250..-$600 predicted, -$234. Predictions were honest.
- Not a pass, not a nothing: the pre-FOMC night alone (8/yr) is the one subset with gross >> cost. It is 3A-5's territory
  (queued as "expect FAIL post-2015"); 3A-5 should be run as the one-shot on that subset with the post-2015 decay as the question.
