# PREREG 3A-5 - The pre-FOMC night, 2016-2023: did the Lucca-Moench drift survive its publication?
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached nights + calendars/fomc.csv (scheduled decisions). Nothing bought.
Context (declared, not evidence): 3A-6 reported the night into the decision at +14.1 bp (ES, 107 nights, 2010-2023). That full-sample
number is CONTEXT ONLY here. The decisive test is the 2016-2023 sub-period, chosen because Lucca-Moench was published in 2015 and
Kurov, Wolfe & Gilbert (2021) report the drift decayed after that; the question is whether anything is left to trade.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): Lucca & Moench 2015 - the equity premium accrued disproportionately in the 24h before scheduled FOMC
  statements (1994-2011, ~49 bp). Proposed payers: investors who reduce exposure into monetary-policy uncertainty and buy it back
  only once the statement resolves it (uncertainty-resolution premium; Hu, Pan, Wang & Zhu 2022 "premium for heightened
  uncertainty"). If the premium is real compensation it should persist at a reduced size after publication; if it was a
  sample accident it should be ~0 after 2015.
- Source: Lucca & Moench, JF 2015; Kurov, Wolfe & Gilbert 2021 (decay post-2015); Hu, Pan, Wang, Zhu 2022.
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; same night (17:02 buy on the day before the decision, 1% stop, 08:30 sell on the
  decision day, 1 micro). The statement (13:00 CT) is outside the hold; the hold captures the overnight half of the LM window.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; held night := d1 is a scheduled FOMC decision date. ~8/yr; 2016-2023 = 64 nights.
- Rule, exactly: hold ONLY the night into a scheduled decision. Scored series = 2016-01-01..2023-12-31 (decisive, pre-declared).
  2010-2015 and the full sample are reported in notes as context. Direction: HOLD.
- Grid (PBO only): {into-FOMC 2016-23, into-FOMC 2010-15, into-FOMC full, post-FOMC 2016-23, all nights 2016-23}.
- Cost model: fixed ($4.50 MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM round trip).
- Predictions (gross/night vs the round trip; 8 trades/yr):
  * 2016-2023 ES: 5-10 bp/night = $10-22 per MES night at the 2016-23 average level ~3300 (2-5x the $4.50 RT). Roughly half the
    2010-2015 bp (which I expect at 15-25 bp). Decayed but not dead.
  * PF 1.2-1.6 on 64 nights; Sharpe annualised by 8 nights/yr 0.3-0.7; t-stat of the mean ~1.0-1.8; DSR p < 0.95; cost share 20-45%.
  * NQ 2016-23: 8-15 bp = $15-30 vs $3.00 (5-10x), cost share 10-20%. YM like ES. RTY (2017+) noisy.
  * Worst night (MES, 1% stop): -$150 to -$350 (pre-FOMC nights are calm; the stop rarely binds).
  * Post-FOMC 2016-23 night: negative (-10 to -20 bp), as in 3A-6, reported only.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate on the 2016-23 series): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%. Minimum track NOT met
  (64 nights, 8 years of a sparse series): any pass is "reported, not gated" - the honest ceiling for this test is "the drift is
  still there at X bp with t = Y", which decides whether the night goes into the Phase 3 candidate as an overlay.
- What would make me distrust a pass: a 2016-23 mean driven by 2020-03 and 2022 nights (report ex-2020); the 2010-15 bp being
  SMALLER than 2016-23 (that contradicts the published decay and smells like noise); the premium concentrated in the 08:00-08:30
  minutes (then it is the cash-open, not the pre-FOMC window).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL by the gate (min track not met; DSR p 0.76-0.85 < 0.95 at N=25-28), reported not gated - but this is the strongest subset
the program has produced (SCOREBOARD N=25..28), decisive sub-period 2016-2023, 63 nights per symbol:
- ES 21.8 bp = $39.78 per MES night vs $4.50 RT (8.8x); PF 3.74; t-stat of the mean 3.33; worst night -$197; ex-2020-03..05 19.4 bp.
- NQ 27.4 bp = $61.62 vs $3.00 (20.5x), PF 4.10, t 3.20. YM 18.5 bp, PF 2.92, t 3.16. RTY 22.6 bp, PF 2.32, t 2.26 (2017+).
- The post-decision night in the same period is -14.6 to -17.9 bp on every symbol: the pre-announcement run-up reverses after it.
- PRE-REGISTERED DISTRUST CONDITION TRIGGERED: 2010-2015 was WEAK (ES 3.2 bp, YM 3.6 bp; NQ 20 bp) and 2016-2023 strong - the
  opposite of the published decay (Kurov-Wolfe-Gilbert). Either the overnight slice of the LM window behaved differently from
  their close-to-close measure, or 2016-23 is the lucky half. Sixty-three nights cannot tell those apart; the holdout can
  (2024-2026 has ~22 such nights). Predictions: 5-10 bp predicted for 2016-23, 21.8 realised (above range); 2010-15 predicted
  15-25 bp, realised 3.2 (far below). My prior about the time pattern was wrong in both halves.
Status: not a sleeve (8 nights/yr, 63 observations). Candidate OVERLAY for the Phase 3 holdout one-shot: hold the pre-FOMC
night regardless of the vol state. Not re-tested in-sample. The ledger shows no 2024+ data touched.
