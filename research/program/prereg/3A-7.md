# PREREG 3A-7 — Turn-of-month nights: month-end rebalancing flows and the TOM premium in index futures
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached nights + cached day-session table. Nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): pension / target-date / balanced funds rebalance to fixed weights at month end, and payroll-driven
  contributions arrive in the first days of the month: a forced, calendar-driven flow. Etula, Rinne, Suominen & Vaittinen (JF 2020,
  "Dash for Cash") show institutions SELL equities in the days before month end to raise cash for month-end payments and buy back
  after, producing negative returns T-4..T-1 and positive returns around the turn. McConnell & Xu (2008) show the turn-of-month
  (T, T+1..T+3) carries essentially the whole equity premium 1926-2005. The holder who supplies liquidity before the turn and
  carries risk through it is paid by the constrained rebalancers.
- Source: McConnell & Xu 2008 FAJ; Etula, Rinne, Suominen, Vaittinen 2020 JF; Lakonishok & Smidt 1988.
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; the standard night (17:02 -> 08:30, 1% stop, 1 micro). Day-session variants are flat by 15:00.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; trading days from the day table (ES dates). T = last trading day of the month.
- Rule, exactly: hold the night ONLY when its exit date d1 is in {T, T+1, T+2, T+3}. Direction pre-declared: HOLD TOM nights.
  Complement (non-TOM) and the pre-turn window (T-4..T-1 nights, expected weak) reported, not scored.
- Grid (PBO only): {TOM nights, non-TOM nights, T-4..T-1 nights, TOM day session 08:30->14:59, TOM last hour 14:00->14:59, all nights}.
- Cost model: fixed $ (PROGRAM.md §0) AND the §0b bp rule (MES 1.4 / MNQ 0.6 / M2K 2.1 / MYM 1.2 bp); both reported.
- Predictions (gross/night vs the round trip is the question; ~4 nights/month, ~48/yr, ~650 per symbol on ES/NQ/YM, ~300 RTY):
  * ES TOM nights 6-9 bp = $6-9 per MES night at 2010-23 levels (1.3-2x the $4.50 RT); bp rule: net 4.5-7.5 bp, cost share 15-25%.
    Non-TOM nights 2-3 bp. T-4..T-1 nights 0-2 bp (the dash-for-cash signature: weaker than average, probably not negative overnight).
  * PF 1.15-1.30; Sharpe annualised by ~48 nights/yr 0.4-0.7; DSR p at N~36: 0.5-0.9 -> probably a DSR fail with PF and the bp
    cost share passing. NQ similar in bp with cost share < 15%. The day-session and last-hour variants: smaller, ~1-3 bp.
  * Stability: present in both 2010-16 and 2017-23 (a flow effect, not a regime). T+1 the strongest single day.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, TOM-night series per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40% (bp rule also
  reported); >= 400 nights (ES/NQ/YM qualify; RTY ~300 does not).
- What would make me distrust a pass: TOM bp not exceeding non-TOM bp by >= 3 bp; the T-4..T-1 window NOT weaker than average
  (then there is no rebalancing signature and the TOM premium is just the unconditional drift on a few nights); the effect
  absent in NQ (flows are index-wide); concentration in < 10 nights.

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=33..36). TOM nights 4.1 bp on ES vs 3.0 non-TOM (NQ 4.4 vs 4.4, YM 4.1 vs 2.2, RTY 6.2 vs 6.6):
a 1-bp edge at most, not the >= 3 bp the mechanism needed. Gross $3.77 vs $4.50 RT on ES (0.84x); bp rule net 1.9 bp, PF 1.10,
DSR 0.10. PRE-REGISTERED DISTRUST CONDITION TRIGGERED, in the strongest form: the T-4..T-1 window — where the dash-for-cash
selling should make nights WEAK — is the BEST window on every symbol (ES 6.2 bp, NQ 6.4, YM 5.6, RTY 11.9). Whatever the
month-end flow does to cash-equity closes, Globex overnight returns before the turn are not depressed by it. The day-session and
last-hour TOM variants are 1-3 bp. The McConnell-Xu turn-of-month premium is not visible as an overnight-futures subset
2010-2023 at a size that matters. Predictions (6-9 bp, PF 1.15-1.30) were too optimistic. Retired.
