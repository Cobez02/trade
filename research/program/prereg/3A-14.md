# PREREG 3A-14 - Vol-state switch for the overnight sleeve: size 1 or 0 by VIX/VIX3M against an expanding-window threshold
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached nights (ES/NQ/RTY/YM) + CBOE VIX/VIX3M closes. Nothing bought.
Honesty note: the choice of state variable and of the 33rd-percentile cut is INFORMED BY 3A-3 on this same sample (the bottom
tercile of VIX/VIX3M earned nothing). This is therefore an in-sample confirmation of a rule's mechanics, not independent
evidence; the independent evidence is the Phase 3 one-shot holdout, which this test does not touch.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): as 3A-3 - the overnight premium is paid for carrying gap risk when near-term hedging demand is
  high; when the VIX curve is in deep contango nobody is paying for it. A switch that stands aside in the calm state removes
  nights with ~zero gross and ~full cost, which is exactly how 3A-1 failed (fixed $4.50 cost against $3.30 gross).
- Source: Johnson 2017 (VIX term-structure slope and risk premia); the program's own 3A-3 (monotonic terciles, robust ex-2020).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; same night (17:02 buy, 1% stop, 08:30 sell, 1 micro). Size 1 or 0, nothing else.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; VIX and VIX3M 15:15 CT closes <= d0 (Friday's for Sunday nights). No holdout.
- Rule, exactly: r_t = VIX_close / VIX3M_close on the last CBOE date <= d0. Threshold thr_t = the 33rd percentile of {r_s : s < t}
  over the EXPANDING window of all prior CBOE dates, requiring >= 250 prior observations (the window begins 2009-09-18, so the
  first tradeable night is ~2010-09-20; nights before that are NOT held and not counted). Hold (size 1) iff r_t >= thr_t;
  otherwise size 0. One state variable, one threshold, no other condition. The threshold uses only dates strictly before t.
- Grid (PBO only): expanding percentile q in {20, 33, 50, 67} and the unconditional sleeve; headline q = 33, declared here.
- Cost model: fixed ($4.50 MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM round trip), charged only on held nights.
- Predictions (trades/yr and gross/night vs the round trip are what the test reports):
  * Held share ~62-70% of nights after warm-up: ~160-175 nights/yr on ES (~2,150 of ~3,400 eligible).
  * ES held nights: 4.3-5.3 bp/night = $4.3-5.6 per MES night at the 2010-23 average level (0.95-1.25x the $4.50 RT); cost share
    80-105% -> expected FAIL on cost share; PF 1.03-1.12; Sharpe (annualised by ~165 nights/yr) 0.25-0.5; DSR p < 0.95 at N >= 21.
    Nights stood aside: -1 to +1 bp.
  * NQ held: 5.5-7 bp = $5.5-7.5 vs $3.00 RT (1.8-2.5x), cost share 40-55%, PF 1.08-1.18 - the closest to the gate, probably still a
    DSR fail. YM $2.5-3.5 vs $3.00 (0.8-1.2x). RTY noisy.
  * Grid: higher q -> higher bp but fewer nights; q=67 should look like 3A-3's top tercile (~6 bp on ES) at ~85 nights/yr.
    The expanding threshold will lag the state in 2010-11 (short window) and in the 2017 low-vol year.
  * Worst held night (MES): -$230 to -$400 (stress nights are in the held set by construction).
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, held-night series per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 nights (all qualify).
- What would make me distrust a pass: the held set's bp not exceeding the unconditional bp by >= 1.5 (then the switch adds
  nothing but turnover); the result depending on q (PBO > 0.5); any night where the threshold window includes the night's own
  date (checked in code: thr uses shift(1)); the stood-aside set having positive gross > the RT (then standing aside costs money).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=21..24). The switch does its job as a filter and still does not clear the cost line:
- Held share 59-64% (predicted 62-70%); ES 153 held nights/yr, first held night 2010-09-22 (250-observation warm-up as declared).
- ES held nights 5.5 bp = $6.27/night vs $4.50 RT (1.39x; predicted 0.95-1.25x, realised above range); stood-aside nights -0.3 bp
  (-$0.98/night) - the state variable separates the nights cleanly, exactly as 3A-3 implied, with no look-ahead (threshold =
  quantile of strictly prior dates, verified by hand on three dates before the run).
- But net: PF 1.01 (ES), 1.05 (NQ), cost share 72% ES / 38% NQ / 75% YM, Sharpe 0.02-0.18, DSR p 0.03-0.10. The held set's
  variance is the stress-night variance; $6.27 gross against $4.50 cost leaves $1.77 a night on a ~$165 standard deviation.
- Grid: q=50 is better than q=33 on every symbol (ES $7.60, NQ $9.70, YM $5.06) and q=67 is not better than q=50 - the state
  effect is a step, not a gradient, roughly at the median. PBO 0.38-0.75: do not read the q ordering as a finding.
- Predictions: held bp 4.3-5.3 predicted, 5.5 realised; cost share 80-105% predicted, 72%; PF 1.03-1.12, 1.01; stood-aside -1..+1
  bp, -0.3. Right on the shape, slightly too pessimistic on gross, right on the verdict.
Reading: the sizing switch is the right structure for the anchor sleeve (it removes 40% of nights that earn nothing and cost
full freight) but at 2010-2023 index levels a micro still cannot carry the remaining premium. At 2024+ levels (ES ~5500,
2x the 2010-23 average) the same 5.5 bp is ~$15/night against $4.50 - that is the Phase 3 holdout question, not an in-sample one.
