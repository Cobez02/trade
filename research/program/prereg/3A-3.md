# PREREG 3A-3 — Overnight conditioned on the VIX term structure: hold when the curve is flat/inverted (VIX >= VIX3M)
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running (after 3A-6/3A-4 outcomes; before any 3A-2 result). Data: cached nights + CBOE VIX/VIX3M closes.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the overnight premium is compensation for carrying gap risk that hedgers will not carry. The VIX
  term-structure slope measures how much is being paid for near-term protection relative to longer-dated: when VIX >= VIX3M
  (flat or inverted, backwardation) near-term hedging demand is high and the variance risk premium for the next sessions is
  largest, so the expected return to the risk-bearer per night is largest. In deep contango (calm) the premium per night is
  small. Payers: option buyers and vol-targeting funds de-risking in stress.
- Source: Johnson 2017 JFE "Risk premia and the VIX term structure" (the slope prices variance and equity risk premia);
  Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou 2009 (variance risk premium predicts returns); Cheng 2019 (VIX premium and hedging demand).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session, same night as 3A-1. Signal = the 15:15 CT VIX and VIX3M closes on d0 (Friday's for
  Sunday nights), known 1h45m before the 17:02 entry. No look-ahead.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; calendars/vix_daily.csv, vix3m_daily.csv (CBOE, <= 2023). ratio r = VIX / VIX3M.
- Rule, exactly: hold the standard night (17:02 buy, 1% stop, 08:30 sell, 1 micro) ONLY when r >= 1.0 on d0. Direction
  pre-declared: HOLD IN BACKWARDATION. Contango nights (r < 1) are the complement (reported, not scored).
- Grid (PBO only): {backwardation r>=1, contango r<1, r terciles low/mid/high (full-sample cut points, declared here), all}.
- Cost model: fixed ($4.50 MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM round trip).
- Predictions (gross $/night in the subset vs the round trip is the question):
  * Backwardation nights: 12-18% of nights (~450-600 on ES, ~40/yr), clustered in 2010-12, 2015-16, 2018Q4, 2020, 2022.
  * ES in backwardation: 6-12 bp/night = $5-10 per MES night (1.1-2.2x the $4.50 RT) with 2-3x the normal night variance:
    PF 1.05-1.20, Sharpe annualised by ~40 nights/yr 0.2-0.5, cost share 45-90% -> expected FAIL (variance and cost share),
    worst MES night -$500 to -$900 (March 2020 is in this subset by construction). NQ $8-15 vs $3.00 (2.5-5x), cost share 20-40%.
  * Contango nights: 2-3 bp, $2-3 per MES night, below the RT; the low-r tercile the weakest in bp but the best in Sharpe.
  * Monotonic in r terciles: high > mid > low in bp. If the mid tercile is the best, the slope is not what is paying.
  * The stop binds often in backwardation (gap-through fills): the 1% stop will cost 20-35% of gross here.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, backwardation subset per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 nights
  (ES/NQ/YM expected to qualify, RTY not).
- What would make me distrust a pass: a pass that vanishes without 2020-03..2020-05; a subset mean driven by < 10 nights; the
  tercile ordering not monotonic; the contango subset showing the same bp (then the slope carries no information); any night
  where the VIX close used is dated AFTER d0 (check the merge: VIX date <= d0 always).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL by the gate on all four (SCOREBOARD N=17..20), but this is the strongest conditioning result of the family and the
only one where every pre-registered check came out the predicted way:
- Backwardation nights (7.4-8.4% of nights, ~21/yr; fewer than the 12-18% predicted) earn 13.5 bp on ES ($13.66 vs $4.50 RT =
  3.0x), 17.2 bp NQ ($20.55, 6.9x), 10.7 bp YM ($9.29, 3.1x), 18.5 bp RTY; contango nights 1.9-3.3 bp, at or below the RT.
- Monotonic in the ratio on every symbol: low tercile -0.9/+0.6/+0.8/-0.0 bp (ES/NQ/RTY/YM), mid 4.2/4.3/6.6/3.7, high 6.2/8.2/12.0/4.1.
  The ENTIRE 2010-2023 overnight premium sits in the top two terciles of VIX/VIX3M; deep contango nights earn nothing.
- Robust to removing 2020-03..05 (ES 14.2 bp on the remaining 219 nights). Not a COVID artefact.
- Why it fails: 253-258 nights is under the 400-night minimum track; the per-night variance in stress is 2-3x normal so the net
  Sharpe is 0.17-0.31 and PF 1.11-1.22 (ES/YM below 1.15); DSR p 0.10-0.21 at N=17-20. Cost share passes (15-33%).
- Predictions: ES $5-10 gross predicted, $13.66 realised (above range); PF 1.05-1.20 predicted, 1.11; Sharpe 0.2-0.5, 0.17;
  worst night -$500..-$900 predicted, -$223 (the stop caps it; the 1% stop costs gross here as predicted).
What this says to the program: the overnight premium is a stress-state premium. The anchor sleeve's failure (3A-1) is the
average of a dead contango state and a live backwardation state. Any Phase 3 candidate from this family should be the
state-conditioned version (3A-14 is the right next test: size by trailing/implied vol state, corrected for look-ahead) and
the sample for it must be lengthened (ES 2010-2023 has only ~260 stress nights). Retired as specified; not re-tested.
