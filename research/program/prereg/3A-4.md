# PREREG 3A-4 — FOMC-cycle even weeks: does the overnight premium follow the bi-weekly Fed cycle?
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running (and before reading 3A-6's scoreboard lines). Data: cached nights table + calendars/fomc.csv.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): Cieslak, Morse & Vissing-Jorgensen (RFS 2019) show the US equity premium 1994-2016 was earned
  entirely in even weeks of the FOMC cycle (weeks 0, 2, 4, 6 counted in weekdays from the scheduled announcement). Their
  mechanism: the Fed's internal bi-weekly cycle (Board/discount-rate meetings, informal communication) releases unexpectedly
  accommodative policy news in even weeks, lowering risk premia; the holder of index risk during those weeks is paid as the
  "Fed put" is re-priced. Payers: investors who de-risk ahead of policy uncertainty and re-enter after it resolves.
- Source: Cieslak, Morse, Vissing-Jorgensen 2019 "Stock Returns over the FOMC Cycle"; replication/decay evidence: the effect
  weakened after 2016 (acknowledged by the authors; Hu et al. attribute the week-0 part to pre-FOMC drift). Honest prior:
  partly real (week 0 = Lucca-Moench), partly a 1994-2016 artefact.
- Venue constraints: prop single-session, same night as 3A-1.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; scheduled FOMC decision dates only (111). No holdout.
- Rule, exactly: FOMC-cycle day t = number of WEEKDAYS since the most recent scheduled announcement (t = 0 on it), except
  that the single weekday before the next announcement is t = -1 (CMVJ convention). week = floor((t + 1) / 5); even weeks =
  {0, 2, 4, 6}. A night belongs to the cycle week of its EXIT date d1 (the return is realised into d1). Hold the standard night
  (17:02 buy, 1% stop, 08:30 sell, 1 micro) ONLY when d1 is in an even week. Direction pre-declared: HOLD even weeks.
- Grid (PBO only): {even, odd, week0, week2, week4, week6, all}; no headline chosen from it.
- Cost model: fixed ($4.50 MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM round trip).
- Predictions (gross $/night in the subset vs the round trip is the question):
  * Even-week nights: ~55-60% of nights (weeks 0,2,4 are always present; 6 is rare) — ~1,950 of 3,468 on ES, ~145/yr.
  * ES even weeks: 4-5 bp/night = $4.0-5.5 per MES night (0.9-1.2x the $4.50 RT); odd weeks 0-2 bp ($0-2). The split should be
    visible (even > odd by >= 2 bp) but the dollars still sit at the cost line: cost share 80-110% -> expected FAIL on cost
    share with PF 1.00-1.10. NQ even weeks $5-7 vs $3.00 RT (1.7-2.3x), cost share 45-60%, also likely a cost-share fail.
  * Sharpe of the even-week series (annualised by ~145 nights/yr): 0.3-0.6. DSR p < 0.95 at N >= 9 (scoreboard count).
  * Week 0 carries the most bp (it contains the pre-FOMC night from 3A-6); weeks 2 and 4 positive but smaller; week 6 noisy.
  * Decay: 2010-2016 even/odd gap larger than 2017-2023. If the gap is ABSENT in 2010-2016 the paper's effect is not in futures.
  * Worst night (MES, 1% stop): -$300 to -$600; skew negative.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, even-week subset, per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%. >= 400 nights: all four qualify.
- What would make me distrust a pass: the pass coming only from week 0 (then it is 3A-6/3A-5, not a cycle effect); the even/odd
  gap flipping sign across 2010-16 vs 2017-23; the effect relying on week-6 nights (tiny sample); any holiday-miscount in the
  weekday-based cycle clock shifting weeks (check: announcement days must all land in week 0 at t = 0).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=9..12), and the sign is WRONG: odd weeks out-earn even weeks in every symbol and in both
sub-periods (ES even/odd bp 2.0/3.7 in 2010-16, 2.5/3.6 in 2017-23; YM 1.2/3.0 and 1.7/3.6; NQ 4.6/5.2 and 3.0/3.7).
Even-week gross/night: ES $2.71 (0.60x the RT), YM $1.43 (0.48x), NQ $4.56 (1.5x), RTY $4.46 (1.5x). Cost share 66-210%.
Week 0 nets to ~zero because the pre-FOMC night (+14 bp, 3A-6) and the post-FOMC night (-15 bp) cancel inside it; week 4 is
the only even week with any premium (5-7 bp), week 2 is dead (0.4-1.5 bp). PBO 0.33-0.94: the week structure is noise.
The CMVJ cycle is not in Globex overnight index returns 2010-2023. Predictions were wrong on the split (I predicted even > odd
by >= 2 bp; the gap is -1 to -2 bp the other way) and right on the gate outcome (cost-share fail). Retired; do not re-test.
The cycle clock was verified before the run: all 111 announcement days land at t=0 / week 0, the weekday before at week 0.
