# PREREG 3A-11 - Crude oil into the EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wed 09:30 CT): a scheduled-release premium in CL?
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE buying CL. Purchase approved by Connor: CL $17.10, which exhausts the Databento credit
(ledger $105.86 -> ~$122.96 of $125; every later request must be refused by the loader).

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the EIA report is the one scheduled, market-moving information release in crude. If holders of
  crude risk are paid for bearing announcement uncertainty (the Savor-Wilson / Ai-Bansal announcement premium, which 3A-6 found
  in equities), the premium should accrue in the window INTO the release and be absent in the same window on other days.
  Payers: producers/refiners hedging into the number and intraday desks that will not carry a 09:30 print.
- Source: Savor & Wilson 2013 (announcement premium, equities); Bu 2014 and Ye & Karali 2016 (oil price reaction to EIA
  inventory surprises - reaction, not drift); Lucca-Moench find no pre-announcement drift in non-equity assets. Honest prior: the
  reaction is symmetric and surprise-driven, so the directional drift is probably ~0 - this test closes the question.
- Venue constraints: prop single-session (CL is allowed; this is a 89-minute intraday hold, flat before the print).
- Data: Databento GLBX.MDP3 ohlcv-1m CL.v.0 2010-06-06..2023-12-31 (the purchase). EIA release day := Wednesday, or Thursday when a
  US federal holiday falls on the Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday of that week (EIA's published convention; the rule is validated
  against the published schedule where it can be fetched, and any mismatch is listed in the outcome).
- Rule, exactly: on EIA days, buy 1 CL at the 08:00 CT open, sell at the 09:29 CT open (before the 09:30 release). No stop
  (89-minute hold, the day's range is the risk). Direction pre-declared: LONG INTO the release. Full-size CL: $1,000/pt;
  cost $25.00 RT ($2.50 + 1 tick per side); bp rule 2.6 bp.
- Grid (PBO only): {EIA-day 08:00->09:29, non-EIA-day 08:00->09:29, EIA-day 09:30->10:30 (reaction, reported only, no direction),
  EIA-day full pit session 08:00->13:29, all days 08:00->09:29}.
- Predictions (gross per trade vs the round trip is the question):
  * ~50 EIA days/yr, ~680 trades. CL 2010-23 average ~$70: 1 bp = $7 per contract.
  * EIA-day 08:00->09:29: -1 to +3 bp = -$7 to +$21 vs $25 RT (< 1x); cost share > 100% or undefined; PF 0.90-1.05; Sharpe -0.2
    to +0.3. FAIL. Non-EIA days in the same window: -1 to +1 bp. If EIA days exceed non-EIA days by >= 3 bp the premium exists
    but is unprofitable at full-size cost; if not, it does not exist.
  * Reaction window 09:30->10:30: |move| 2-3x a normal hour; mean ~0; reported, not scored.
  * Worst trade: -$1,500 to -$3,000 (a 2-3% pre-release move on a 2020 or 2022 day).
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, EIA-day series): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40% (both $ and bp rule reported).
- What would make me distrust a pass: a mean driven by April 2020 (negative-price week) or a handful of 2022 days; the EIA-day
  vs non-EIA-day gap not present in both 2010-16 and 2017-23; Thursday-shifted weeks mis-assigned (count them, list them).

### Addendum, 2026-10-14, still BEFORE the purchase
The published 2025-26 EIA schedule (fetched today; prior years are not online) shows every holiday-shifted Thursday release at
11:00 or 12:00 ET, i.e. 10:00-11:00 CT, NOT 09:30 CT. So the 08:00->09:29 window is "into the release" only on regular
Wednesdays. Primary subset is therefore REGULAR WEDNESDAY releases only (606 days); the 103 shifted Thursdays are reported
separately with an 08:00->09:59 window and are not scored. Everything else above stands.
