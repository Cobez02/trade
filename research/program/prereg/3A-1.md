# PREREG 3A-1 — Globex overnight drift in index futures (anchor sleeve), 2010-06-06..2023-12-31, 1% stop
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE any Databento download. Nothing below is informed by 2010-2023 minute data; the only
prior numbers are the 2024-26 Yahoo-hourly study in research/PREREG-2026-10-08-globex-overnight.md, which is holdout
territory and is NOT reused as evidence here.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the equity premium is earned almost entirely overnight; day-session returns are ~zero.
  Payers: (a) intraday participants (market makers, day-trading funds, prop desks) who are structurally flat by the
  close and pay a premium to shed overnight gap risk; (b) index-arb and hedging demand that leans on futures when cash
  is shut. The futures holder is paid for carrying the gap risk nobody else wants between 17:00 CT and the cash open.
- Source (paper / structural fact): Kelly & Clark 2011 ("Returns in Trading versus Non-Trading Hours"); Lou, Polk &
  Skouras 2019 JFE ("A tug of war: overnight versus intraday expected returns"); Cooper, Cliff & Gulen 2008 ("Return
  differences between trading and non-trading hours"). Structural: Globex single-session rule — a position opened after
  17:00 CT and closed before the next day's cutoff is inside one session, so prop firms allow it.
- Venue constraints it respects: prop single-session (Topstep: flat by 15:10 CT; we are flat by 08:30 CT). Entry 17:02
  CT Sun-Thu, exit 08:30 CT Mon-Fri. No Friday-night entries (Globex closes Friday 16:00 CT; no position may cross).
- Data (≤ 2023-12-31 only; holdout untouched): Databento GLBX.MDP3 ohlcv-1m, continuous front-by-volume (.v.0),
  ES NQ RTY YM, 2010-06-06 (dataset start; the QUEUE said 2010-01-01 but GLBX.MDP3 begins 2010-06-06) .. 2023-12-31.
  RTY only exists on CME from July 2017, so its sample is ~6.5 years. Estimated cost of these four: $58.18 of $125.
  The loader refuses any request that reaches 2024.
- Rule, exactly (entry, exit, stop, sizing): buy 1 micro contract at the open of the 17:02 CT 1-minute bar; rest a
  stop at entry × (1 − 0.01); stop fill = stop level if the bar's low touches it, or the bar's open if the bar opens
  through it (gap); otherwise sell at the open of the 08:30 CT bar next morning. Skip the night if either bar is
  missing. One contract, no sizing, no filters. P&L in micro dollars (MES $5/pt, MNQ $2/pt, M2K $5/pt, MYM $0.50/pt).
- Parameters and the pre-declared grid: stop ∈ {0, 0.75%, 1%, 1.5%, 2%}. The 1% stop is the pre-declared headline
  (it is the live rule). The grid exists only to report PBO; the headline is not chosen from it.
- Cost model: fixed (see PROGRAM.md §0): micro = $1.00 commission + 1 tick slippage per side
  (MES $2.25/side, MNQ $1.50, M2K $1.50, MYM $1.50), i.e. $4.50 / $3.00 / $3.00 / $3.00 round trip.
- Predictions:
  * trades/year: ~245 nights per symbol (Sun-Thu minus holidays and missing bars); ~3,300 nights for ES/NQ/YM,
    ~1,600 for RTY.
  * Sharpe (annualised, net, 1% stop): ES 0.4–0.9, NQ 0.5–1.0, YM 0.4–0.9, RTY 0.2–0.7. Point estimate ES ≈ 0.6.
    Lower than the 2024-26 1.1 because (i) 2010-era index levels are a quarter of today's, so the same %-drift is
    fewer dollars against a fixed $-cost, and (ii) 2024-26 was an unusually calm uptrend.
  * Stop effect: the 1% stop should cost ~10-20% of gross drift and improve worst-night but not Sharpe; expect the
    0% and 2% variants to have Sharpe within ±0.15 of the 1% variant. If the 1% stop is the BEST of the grid by a
    wide margin, that is suspicious, not good.
  * Worst night (MES, 1% stop): −$400 to −$900. The stop cannot save a limit-down reopen (March 2020: ES locked
    −5% at the 17:00 open; a gap through the stop fills at the open). Expect 2-5 nights worse than −$300 (MES).
  * Cost share (costs / gross): 30–55%. This is the gate most likely to FAIL on 2010-2023: average gross drift per
    night in MES dollars is probably $6–10 against $4.50 of cost. A fail on cost share alone, with DSR passing,
    means the sleeve is fine at 2020+ index levels but was not tradeable in micros a decade ago; log it as such.
  * Profit factor: 1.10–1.30. Win rate ~53-56%.
  * Correlation with existing sleeves: ES/NQ/YM overnight P&L mutually correlated 0.7–0.9 (one sleeve, four tickers,
    not four sleeves). No other sleeve exists yet in the program; the intraday QQQ paper sleeve is not scored.
  * Skew: negative (stops cap the left tail at −1% most nights but gap nights get through).
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate): deflated Sharpe p ≥ 0.95 at N=1..4 trials, PF ≥ 1.15, cost share < 40%,
  worst night inside the Topstep $50K floor (MES single-night loss well under $2,000 daily-loss limit; MNQ too).
  Judged per symbol; ES is the anchor. The sleeve gets to Phase 3 if ES passes; NQ/YM/RTY passing adds nothing but
  confidence (they are one bet).
- What would make me distrust a pass:
  * Sharpe > 1.2 on 13 years in a micro after costs — too good for a premium the literature puts near 0.6-0.9 gross.
  * The 1% stop variant being the best of the grid by > 0.2 Sharpe (parameter luck; PBO > 0.5 would confirm).
  * Gross drift concentrated in < 5% of nights (a handful of 2020 reopens driving the mean).
  * Session bars at 17:02 that are not actually the reopen (roll artefacts in .v.0 around expiry weeks: the
    continuous series switches contract by volume, so a 17:02 "entry" on roll day may be in a different contract
    than the 08:30 "exit"; check the P&L on the 8 quarterly roll weeks/year separately; if they dominate, redo
    with per-contract stitching).
  * Any 2024+ data touched (the loader should make this impossible; the ledger will show it if it happened).
  * Cost share passing only because gross is inflated by roll gaps.

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14 — everything above was fixed before any data was bought)
FAIL on all four symbols (SCOREBOARD N=1..4). Net Sharpe at the 1% stop: ES -0.37, NQ 0.03, RTY -0.31, YM -0.23;
cost share ES 136%, YM 126%, NQ 69%, RTY 65%. The drift itself is there: gross 17:02->08:30 return is +3.2 bp/night

with the literature. It fails because at 2010-2023 index levels (ES averaged 2585) +3.2 bp is $3.30/night in an MES
against $4.50 of fixed round-trip cost. The premium is real and thin; the micro contract could not carry it for most
of the sample. My prereg predicted cost share 30-55% and gross $6-10/night: I overestimated gross by ~2x.
Distrust checks: the volume-roll artefact does not rescue or sink it (nights with a >40 bp one-minute jump — rolls
plus crash nights — contribute positively; excluding them lowers gross). No 2024+ data touched (ledger).
Implication for the live plan: at ES ~6000, +3.2 bp/night is ~$9.60 gross, ~$5 net per MES night, so the honest
expectation for the Combine sleeve is Sharpe ~0.4-0.6, not the 1.1 measured on 2024-26 (two unusually good years).
