# PREREG 3A-13 - Cross-market lead-lag at the open: ES overnight move -> RTY/YM catch-up 08:30 -> 09:00
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached ES nights (the signal) + cached RTY/YM day tables (the trade). Nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): large-cap prices incorporate overnight information first; small-cap and Dow-component prices catch
  up with a lag (Lo & MacKinlay 1990 lead-lag; Hou 2007 industry/size information diffusion). If the lag survives in the FUTURES
  (RTY, YM) the small-cap future should continue in the direction of the ES overnight move after the 08:30 open. Honest prior:
  the documented lag is in cash stocks over days, and index futures are arbitraged against ES within seconds; the lag at the
  futures level is probably gone. Payer, if any: slow cash-market participants whose orders arrive after the open.
- Source: Lo & MacKinlay 1990 RFS; Hou 2007 RFS; Chordia & Swaminathan 2000.
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; 08:30 -> 09:00 CT, both legs inside the day session.
- Data: ES nights table (ret 17:02 -> 08:30, known at 08:30), RTY day table 2017-07..2023 (~1,600 days), YM 2010-2023.
- Rule, exactly: at the 08:30 open take 1 M2K in the direction of sign(ES overnight return); exit at the 09:00 open. No stop.
  Direction pre-declared: FOLLOW ES. YM (MYM) is the second symbol.
- Grid (PBO only): {follow exit 09:00, 08:45, 09:30, 10:00; follow 09:00 on |ES overnight| > 30 bp; fade 09:00}.
- Cost model: fixed $ and §0b bp (M2K 2.1 bp, MYM 1.2 bp); both reported.
- Predictions (gross per trade vs the round trip):
  * RTY ~1,600 trades (2017-23), YM ~3,300. Per-trade gross 0 to +2 bp = $0-1 per M2K trade vs $3.00 RT; bp rule: net -2 to 0 bp,
    cost share > 100%. PF 0.95-1.05; Sharpe -0.3 to +0.2. Expected FAIL. Big-signal days (|ES overnight| > 30 bp) +1 to +3 bp.
  * Longer exits (09:30, 10:00) do not improve the mean (the 30-minute window already contains the whole first-hour reaction).
  * YM weaker than RTY in bp (Dow components are large caps).
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 trades (both qualify).
- What would make me distrust a pass: the edge only in 2020; fade and follow both positive on different exits (noise); the edge
  entirely in the 08:30-08:45 minutes (that is the opening auction, not a lag).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on both (SCOREBOARD N=47..48). RTY follow 08:30->09:00: -0.3 bp (1,615 trades); 08:45 exit -0.0 bp; 09:30 and 10:00 exits
-2.9 bp (the first hour REVERSES the overnight ES move in small caps, mildly). Big-signal days +0.9 bp. YM follow +0.9 bp at 09:00,
cost 1.2 bp at the bp rule: net -0.3 bp, PF 0.97. No lead-lag survives at the futures level, as the honest prior said: RTY and YM
futures are repriced with ES overnight, and what is left after 08:30 is a small reversal. Predictions (0..+2 bp, FAIL) right. Retired.
