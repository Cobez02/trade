# PREREG 3A-2 - Overnight conditioned on the prior day session: hold after DOWN days (intraday-overnight tug of war)
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running (after 3A-6 and the 3A-4 prereg; before any 3A-4 result). Data: cached nights table only.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the day session is where liquidity is DEMANDED (dealer delta hedging into a down move, leveraged-ETF
  end-of-day rebalancing that sells after down days - Cheng & Madhavan 2009 - and intraday desks cutting risk into the close).
  Those sellers pay a concession; the close sits below fair value after a down day, and the overnight holder earns the ordinary
  premium plus the reversal of that concession. Lou, Polk & Skouras (JFE 2019) document the persistent negative relation between
  intraday and subsequent overnight returns ("tug of war") at the stock level; at the index level the same shows as overnight
  rebounds after intraday selloffs. After UP days the end-of-day flow buys, so the close is rich and the night earns less.
- Source: Lou, Polk, Skouras 2019; Cheng & Madhavan 2009 (leveraged ETF rebalancing); Bogousslavsky 2021 (end-of-day price
  pressure and overnight reversal).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session, same night as 3A-1. The signal is complete at 14:59 CT; entry 17:02.
- Data: nights 2010-06-06..2023-12-31; day_ret = close of the 14:59 bar / open of the 08:30 bar - 1 for the most recent completed
  day session before entry (Friday's for Sunday nights). No holdout.
- Rule, exactly: hold the standard night (17:02 buy, 1% stop, 08:30 sell, 1 micro) ONLY when day_ret < 0. Direction pre-declared:
  HOLD AFTER DOWN DAYS; up-day nights are the complement (reported, not scored).
- Grid (PBO only): {down, up, bottom tercile of day_ret, middle tercile, top tercile, all}. Terciles computed on the full
  2010-2023 sample (a mild in-sample choice; declared here so PBO carries it). No headline chosen from the grid.
- Cost model: fixed ($4.50 MES, $3.00 MNQ/M2K/MYM round trip).
- Predictions (gross $/night in the subset vs the round trip is the question):
  * Down-day nights: ~46-48% of nights (~1,600 on ES, ~120/yr).
  * ES after down days: 4.5-6.5 bp/night = $4.5-7 per MES night (1.0-1.5x the $4.50 RT); after up days 0-2 bp ($0-2). Cost share
    65-100% -> likely cost-share FAIL; PF 1.05-1.15; Sharpe (annualised by ~120 nights/yr) 0.3-0.6. NQ $6-9 vs $3.00 (2-3x),
    cost share 35-50% - the one with a chance at the gate; DSR p probably < 0.95 at N >= 13.
  * Bottom tercile (worst days) stronger in bp (6-9) but with the highest variance and the worst nights (March 2020 lives here):
    worst MES night -$400 to -$900. Skew negative.
  * Monotonic: bottom > middle > top tercile in bp. If the middle tercile is the best, the mechanism is not what is paying.
  * Stability: the down/up gap should be present in both 2010-16 and 2017-23 (it is a microstructure effect, not a regime).
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate, down-day subset per symbol): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 nights (all qualify).
- What would make me distrust a pass: a pass that disappears when 2020-03 is removed; the gap confined to the extreme tercile
  with nothing in the mild-down nights; the effect in ES but absent in NQ/YM (the end-of-day flow is index-wide);
  a conditioning bar (14:59) missing on early-close days creating a biased subset (count the NaNs).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=13..16). ES/NQ show the predicted direction but too small to matter: down-day nights 4.2 bp vs
up-day 2.3 bp on ES (gross $4.14 vs $4.50 RT = 0.92x), NQ 5.4 vs 3.5 ($5.18 vs $3.00, 1.73x, cost share 58%). YM and RTY go
the other way (YM 2.0 vs 3.1 bp; RTY 4.6 vs 8.3). Pre-registered distrust condition triggered: the ordering is NOT monotonic -
the MIDDLE tercile of day_ret is the best subset on ES, NQ and RTY, and the bottom (worst-day) tercile is the worst net on every
symbol. The end-of-day-flow reversal is not what pays here; the "down > up" gap on ES/NQ is a mild-day effect with no mechanism.
Predictions: down-day ES gross $4.5-7 predicted, $4.14 realised (below range); cost share 65-100% predicted, 109%; worst night
-$400..-$900 predicted, -$237 (the 1% stop caps it). Retired; do not re-test.
