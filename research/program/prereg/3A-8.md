# PREREG 3A-8 — Opex-week last-30-minute reversal (dealer gamma pinning) and the quarterly roll week
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached day-session table (ES/NQ/RTY/YM). Nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): in the week of monthly index-option expiry (third Friday) dealers' net gamma is largest and most
  concentrated near at-the-money strikes; hedging a long-gamma book means selling into rallies and buying dips, which damps
  late-day moves ("pinning"). A trader who fades the day's move in the last 30 minutes is paid by the hedgers' flow. After
  expiry that gamma rolls off; the following week's moves are less damped (continuation). The quarterly roll weeks (Mar/Jun/
  Sep/Dec) add futures-roll flow on top. The September program note: UNCONDITIONAL last-30-min momentum FAILED; this test
  conditions on week type as the mechanism demands.
- Source: Ni, Pearson & Poteshman 2005 (stock pinning at expiration); Baltussen, Da, Lammers & Martens 2021 (hedging demand and
  intraday momentum, the opposite sign when dealers are short gamma); Barbon & Buraschi 2021 (gamma fragility).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session; 14:30 -> 14:59 CT, flat before 15:00.
- Data: day table 2010-06-06..2023-12-31. Day move m = o_1430 / o_0830 - 1. Opex week = Mon..Fri containing the third Friday.
- Rule, exactly: in opex weeks, at 14:30 take the position OPPOSITE to sign(m) (fade), exit at the 14:59 close. No stop. 1 micro.
  Direction pre-declared: FADE in opex week. Follow in the post-opex week and fade in other weeks are grid variants.
- Grid (PBO only): {opex fade, opex follow, post-opex follow, other-weeks fade, all-days follow, quarterly-roll-week fade}.
- Cost model: fixed $ and §0b bp (MES 1.4 bp etc.); both reported.
- Predictions (gross per trade vs the round trip):
  * ~60 trades/yr per symbol (5 days x 12), ~800 total on ES. Mean |last-30-min move| 12-20 bp.
  * Opex-week fade: +1 to +3 bp per trade gross = $1-3 per MES trade vs $4.50 RT (< 1x at 2010-23 levels); bp rule: net -0.5 to
    +1.5 bp, cost share 50-140%. PF 0.95-1.10; Sharpe (annualised by 60) -0.2 to +0.4. Expected FAIL on every gate.
  * Post-opex follow: +0 to +2 bp. Other-weeks fade: ~0. If opex fade is not better than other-weeks fade by >= 1 bp the week
    structure carries nothing. Quarterly roll weeks: noisier, same sign.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 trades (ES/NQ/YM qualify).
- What would make me distrust a pass: the edge living in the 2010-12 or 2020 high-vol weeks only; the sign flipping between
  2010-16 and 2017-23; a pass on one symbol only; opex "fade" and "all-days follow" both positive (then it is the sample's
  last-30-min variance, not the mechanism).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on all four (SCOREBOARD N=37..40), with the sign WRONG: the opex-week FADE loses -0.9 bp per trade on ES (-1.2 NQ, -0.7 RTY,
-0.4 YM), i.e. the last 30 minutes in opex week CONTINUE the day's move, as they do in every other week (all-days follow +1.3 bp,
post-opex follow +1.4 bp, other-weeks fade -1.5 bp). There is no pinning signature in the last half hour of index futures in
expiry week; quarterly roll weeks are the same. Mean |last-30-min move| 17-24 bp. Net of costs every variant loses (the
unconditional follow at +1.3 bp gross is below the 1.4 bp MES round trip; NQ at 1.2 bp gross vs 0.6 bp cost nets ~0.6 bp/trade,
PF ~1.0). Predictions (+1..+3 bp for the fade) were wrong in sign; the gate prediction (FAIL) was right. Retired.
