# PREREG 3A-12 - Opening-range breakout on MNQ, un-leveraged (Zarattini-Aziz), closed properly on 13.5 years
Written 2026-10-14 BEFORE running. Data: cached day table (NQ primary, ES second). Nothing bought.

- Mechanism (who pays, why): the claimed mechanism is that the first minutes of the cash session reveal the day's dominant
  order imbalance (overnight news being absorbed), and that imbalance persists through the day (intraday momentum from the
  open). Zarattini & Aziz (2023, "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?") report a 5-minute ORB on TQQQ 2016-2023 with Sharpe
  ~2 using 4x leverage, an ATR-scaled stop and a profit target. The program's prior (PROGRAM.md §3A.12): thin; close it.
  Honest mechanism assessment: no identified payer - the first 5-minute candle is as likely to be a liquidity overshoot
  (reversal) as information (continuation).
- Source: Zarattini & Aziz 2023 (SSRN); Gao, Han, Li, Zhou 2018 (first-half-hour predicts last-half-hour, NOT the whole day).
- Venue constraints: prop single-session, flat at 14:59. Un-leveraged: 1 micro.
- Data: day table 2010-06-06..2023-12-31: first 5-minute candle (08:30-08:34), its high/low, opens at 08:35, closes at 14:59.
- Rule, exactly: direction = sign of the first 5-min candle (close - open; flat = no trade). Enter at the 08:35 open. Stop = the
  5-min range's opposite extreme (filled at the level if touched, at the bar's open if gapped through). Exit at the 14:59 close if
  not stopped. No target. 1 MNQ (primary), 1 MES (second). The paper's leverage, ATR stop and target are NOT used: the question
  is whether the raw breakout has an edge in futures.
- Grid (PBO only): {NQ orb5, NQ orb15, NQ orb5 long-only, ES orb5}.
- Cost model: fixed $ and §0b bp (MNQ 0.6 bp, MES 1.4 bp); both reported.
- Predictions (gross per trade vs the round trip):
  * ~245 trades/yr, ~3,300 on NQ. Mean 5-min range 15-30 bp. Stop-out rate 55-70% (the stop sits one range away).
  * Per-trade gross: -3 to +3 bp (median -10 to -20 bp from the stop-outs, mean rescued by trend days); PF 0.90-1.08; Sharpe -0.3
    to +0.3; cost share undefined or > 100%. Expected FAIL. Long-only no better after 2010-23's uptrend is netted against stops.
  * orb15: fewer stop-outs, similar mean. ES: same shape, lower bp.
  * By year: positive in 2020 and 2022 (trend days), negative in the pinned years 2013-14, 2017, 2019.
- Pass criteria (PROGRAM.md §1 gate): DSR p >= 0.95, PF >= 1.15, cost share < 40%; >= 400 trades (qualifies).
- What would make me distrust a pass: the whole mean in 2020 and 2022; a pass that disappears with a 1-tick worse entry (breakout
  entries pay the spread twice); long-only passing while the symmetric rule fails (that is the drift, not the breakout).

## Outcome (written AFTER the run, 2026-10-14)
FAIL on both (SCOREBOARD N=45..46). NQ ORB-5: +2.5 bp per trade gross on 3,331 trades, stop-out rate 77% (predicted 55-70%; the
5-min range averages 30 bp and the stop sits one range away), gross $3.83 vs $3.00 RT (1.28x), bp rule net +1.9 bp, PF 1.11,
DSR 0.33. ES ORB-5: +1.2 bp, PF 0.98 at the bp rule, DSR 0.006. ORB-15 is worse on both (NQ +0.6 bp). Long-only NQ +3.2 bp -
the breakout's positive mean on NQ is mostly the 2010-23 Nasdaq uptrend expressed through long days (the pre-registered
"that is the drift" reading); the symmetric rule on ES is flat. By year the NQ mean is positive in 10 of 14 years with no
regime pattern (2018 +8.9, 2022 +7.4, 2020 -2.3). The paper's Sharpe ~2 on TQQQ is not in the un-leveraged futures rule:
PF 1.11 before any slippage on a breakout entry that pays the spread twice. Closed, as the catalog said. Retired.
